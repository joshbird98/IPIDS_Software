# Glossary

The terms used in SINDIE's software and in this help, in alphabetical order, each with the page that covers it.

| Term | Meaning | See |
|---|---|---|
| acknowledge | Mark an alarm as seen, with **Acknowledge selected** or **ACK ALL** in the **Alarms** panel; it then stops sounding. Acknowledging doesn't reset a PLC fault: only **RESET FAULTS** does. | [Alarms](panels/alarms.md#acknowledging) |
| alarm | A condition the alarm service watches and announces in the **Alarms** panel, with a priority and, unless it is low priority, a sound. The rules are in `config/alarm_config.json`; every PLC fault is an alarm too. | [Alarm handling](how-it-works/alarms.md) |
| alarm priority | How urgent an alarm is: critical, high, medium or low. An unacknowledged critical, high or medium alarm sounds the horn; a low one doesn't. | [Alarm handling](how-it-works/alarms.md#priorities) |
| analyser magnet | The mass analyser magnet, whose current selects the mass of the ions that go on down the beamline. Its supply is run by `service_magnet_psu` over Modbus, with hard caps of 95 A and 25 V. | [Beamline Optics](panels/beamline-optics.md) |
| aperture ladder | The ladder of apertures, set by hand: fully retracted is the largest opening. The software doesn't read or set it. | [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md#typical-values) |
| arc detection | The Spellman service's check on its supplies: an output that falls below 10 % of its setpoint, or a current above 500 µA, on 2 polls in a row, is an arc. It stays latched until a fault reset, and the PLC latches it as `Unit<n>_Arc_Exceeded`. | [Services and messaging](how-it-works/services.md#spellman-arc-detection) |
| arc quench | The PLC's answer to an arc on the extraction, target (cathode) or thermionic supply: the applied setpoint goes to 0 for 1 s (control mode 4, badge **ARC QUENCH**), then back. When a supply's arc count reaches 3 (it falls by 1 every 10 s), its `_Sustained_Arc` fault latches and starts a hardware abort. | [The PLC program](how-it-works/plc.md#arc-quench) |
| arming | The first click on a button that switches something on: the button turns amber and asks to confirm (for example **CONFIRM EXTRACTION?**), and only a second click within 4 s sends the command. Switching off takes one click. | [How the controls behave](getting-started/controls-and-colours.md#switching-an-output-on-takes-two-clicks) |
| auto-emission | A PLC control loop that adjusts the filament current, between 20 A and 36 A, to hold the thermionic current at its setpoint. **AUTO EMISSION** in **TESS Ion Source Control** switches it on, and the filament's badge then reads **AUTO-EMIS**. | [The PLC program](how-it-works/plc.md#auto-emission) |
| Automation bar | The bar at the top of the HMI that shows which sequence holds which controls (`<purpose> holds: <subsystems>`), with a **TAKE CONTROL** button for each. It reads `Automation: nothing holds any controls` when none does. | [The Automation bar](panels/automation-bar.md) |
| BACKOFF | The state of a service that the service manager is waiting to restart after a failure: after 2, 5, 10, 30, 60 and 120 s, then every 5 minutes. | [Service Manager](panels/service-manager.md#automatic-restarts) |
| beacon | The traffic-light beacon the PLC drives: red while the safety relay is closed, green while it is open, and amber while a fault in fault word 0, 1, 2, 4 or 5 is latched. A buzzer sounds for 3 s when the relay closes. | [The PLC program](how-it-works/plc.md#beacons-and-buzzer) |
| bleed resistor | The 50 MΩ resistor on the extraction supply and on the target supply. With no beam, their current is the current through it: 0.02 mA per kV. | [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md#typical-values) |
| caesium oven | The oven that heats the source's caesium, shown as **Cesium oven** in **TESS Ion Source Control**. The PLC holds its temperature, typically 100 to 150 °C, and can cool it with compressed air (forced cooling). | [TESS Ion Source Control](panels/ion-source.md) |
| cascaded mode | PLC control mode 3: the filament's setpoint comes from the auto-emission loop instead of from the PC. | [The PLC program](how-it-works/plc.md#control-modes) |
| cathode | See **target**. | [TESS Ion Source Control](panels/ion-source.md) |
| chunk | A file of logged data that the logger writes every 15 minutes to `data/parquet_logs/` (`chunk_<date>_<time>.parquet`). Each night the compactor merges a past day's chunks into a day file. | [Data logging and storage](how-it-works/data-logging.md#chunks) |
| command | A request to a service to change something, such as a setpoint or a switch, from the HMI or a sequence. The service answers with the result and, if it refuses, the reason; every command is also written to the event database. | [Services and messaging](how-it-works/services.md#commands) |
| Command Log | The HMI panel that lists the commands sent from the HMI and their results. Commands sent by recipes, scans and the optimiser aren't in it. | [Command Log](panels/command-log.md) |
| comms fail | A device its service can't reach. The service reports the device's values as unknown and the alarm `<device>: communication lost` is raised; for the source turbo pump, the Spellman supplies, the analyser magnet and the GRAPHIX controllers a `_Comms_Fail` PLC fault latches as well. | [Messages and what they mean](messages.md#plc-faults) |
| compactor | The service (`service_data_compactor`) that at 02:00 each night merges each past day's chunks into a day file with its rollups, and copies the day files, a snapshot of the events and the exports to OneDrive. | [Data logging and storage](how-it-works/data-logging.md#the-nightly-compaction) |
| config editor | The HMI's editor for the configuration files, opened with **Config** in the menu bar and the engineering password. It checks a change before saving it, and keeps earlier versions to go back to. | [Configuration files and the config editor](engineering/configuration.md#the-config-editor) |
| control lease | A sequence's hold on a subsystem while a recipe, a mass scan or the optimiser drives it, renewed every second and lost after 5 s without renewal. While it is held, the services refuse other commands to that subsystem; **TAKE CONTROL** takes it back. | [The Automation bar](panels/automation-bar.md#control-leases) |
| control mode | Who drives a PLC supply: 0 manual (the PC), 1 PLC auto, 2 recipe, 3 cascaded, 4 arc quench. The PLC service refuses most commands to a supply whose mode is above 0, and a badge beside the supply shows modes 1 to 4. | [The PLC program](how-it-works/plc.md#control-modes) |
| crash journal | The logger's file of rows not yet in a chunk (`logger_journal.bin`), added to every 5 s. After a crash the logger recovers those rows from it when it starts again. | [Data logging and storage](how-it-works/data-logging.md#the-crash-journal) |
| current monitor | The ADAM-6017 module that measures the currents on the object and image slits and on the Faraday cup's front aperture. Its service isn't started at present: the module is broken. | [Current Monitoring](panels/current-monitoring.md) |
| data viewer | The program that plots the logged data, live or from any time in the past, with events marked, and exports data and figures (`python src/gui/data_viewer/viewer_main.py`). | [Using the data viewer](viewer/data-viewer.md) |
| day file | One day's logged data, `data/daily_logs/day_<date>.parquet`, made by the compactor from that day's chunks, with its 1-minute and 20-minute rollups beside it. | [Data logging and storage](how-it-works/data-logging.md#the-nightly-compaction) |
| DB10 | The PLC data block the PC and the PLC share (`DB_PC_Interface`): the PLC writes its readbacks, states and fault words there, and the PC its commands, setpoints and the status of the instruments. The PLC service reads it every 100 ms, with the layout in `plc/generated/PLC_PC_Interface.scl`. | [The PLC program](how-it-works/plc.md#db10-and-db11) |
| DB11 | The PLC's retained data block (`DB_PC_Retain`), kept through a power cycle: the latched fault words, and the source supplies' requested setpoints and control modes. | [The PLC program](how-it-works/plc.md#db10-and-db11) |
| degauss | Taking the analyser magnet through 50, 0, 40, 0, 30, 0, 20, 0, 10, 0, 5 and 0 A, 10 s each (`config/magnet_config.json`), with **DEGAUSS ROUTINE** in **Beamline Optics**. The magnet has hysteresis, so degauss before setting a current from above. | [Beamline Optics](panels/beamline-optics.md#degaussing-the-magnet) |
| draft | Changes to a recipe proposed by the recipe builder's drafting, from what you describe: shown in the steps as changes to keep or leave out, and checked like any recipe. The model behind it never acts on the machine. | [Drafting and reviewing with AI](panels/recipes.md#drafting-and-reviewing-with-ai) |
| einzel lens | An electrostatic lens on the beam, powered by a Spellman supply. The source einzel is in **TESS Ion Source Control**; the beamline einzel's row in **Beamline Optics** isn't configured yet. | [TESS Ion Source Control](panels/ion-source.md) |
| emergency switch-off (ESO) | A red mushroom button wired directly into the safety relay's loop: pressing it opens the relay and so removes the high voltage, whatever the software or the PLC do. There are two: on the source deck's side panel and on the **High Voltage Systems Control** box. Test each one every week. | [Safety rules](safety.md#emergency-switch-off) |
| event | A time-stamped record in the event database, `data/events.db`: commands, PLC faults, alarms, gaps in the logging, readbacks out of tolerance, phases, markers and configuration changes. The data viewer draws some kinds on its plots. | [Data logging and storage](how-it-works/data-logging.md#the-events-database) |
| experiment phase | A named stage of an experiment, set with **SET NEW PHASE** in **Experiment Logging**. It is saved as a PHASE_CHANGE event, which the data viewer shows under **Phase Changes**. | [Experiment Logging](panels/experiment-logging.md) |
| extraction | The source's extraction supply, set in kV in **TESS Ion Source Control**. The beam energy, for singly charged ions, is the extraction voltage plus the target voltage. | [TESS Ion Source Control](panels/ion-source.md) |
| Faraday cup | The cup that collects the beam to measure its current, moved into and out of the beam by compressed air. The SMU biases it and measures the current. | [Faraday Cup & SMU](panels/faraday-cup-smu.md) |
| fault | A condition the PLC has latched, such as `Src_Coolant_Flow_Low`. It stays latched, even after its cause has gone, until a fault reset; meanwhile it is listed under **PLC faults** in **System Health** and as an alarm. | [Alarms and faults](procedures/alarms-and-faults.md) |
| fault policy | A recipe's stop conditions, as the recipe file calls them. | [Recipe Execution and the recipe builder](panels/recipes.md#stop-conditions) |
| fault reset | Clearing the PLC's latched faults with **RESET FAULTS** in **System Health**; it also clears the Spellman service's arc latches. The PLC refuses it during some shutdown steps and while the PC watchdog is lost, and a fault whose cause remains latches again. | [System Health](panels/system-health.md#resetting-faults) |
| fault word | One of the six words in which the PLC latches its faults, one bit each: 0 System, 1 Pumps, 2 Source, 3 Spellman, 4 Magnet, 5 Gauges (`config/fault_config.json`). | [Messages and what they mean](messages.md#plc-faults) |
| filament | The source's filament supply, set in A in **TESS Ion Source Control**, typically 28 to 30 A. A new filament, or one after the source has been at air, first degasses at 15 A. | [TESS Ion Source Control](panels/ion-source.md) |
| first-out | The first PLC trip alarm since the first-out was last reset, marked with a star in the **Alarms** panel, so that the cause of a cascade of trips stands out. **Reset first-out** clears the mark. | [Alarms](panels/alarms.md#first-out) |
| forced cooling | Cooling the caesium oven with compressed air. **FORCED COOLING** in **TESS Ion Source Control** asks for it and then reads **FORCED COOLING: ON**; the oven's second line says **cooling air on** whenever the air is on, whoever asked. The PLC also turns the air on in a shutdown and whenever the safety relay is off. It must be off for the oven to heat. | [TESS Ion Source Control](panels/ion-source.md) |
| gate valve | The source gate valve, opened and closed by the PLC from the **Vacuum System** panel. It opens only with the PLC's open permissive, and closing it opens the safety relay. | [Vacuum System](panels/vacuum.md#source-gate-valve) |
| GRAPHIX | The two Leybold GRAPHIX controllers that read the vacuum gauges VG1 to VG6, run by `service_vac_gauge_controllers`. | [Vacuum System](panels/vacuum.md#vacuum-gauges) |
| hard cap | A limit fixed in the code (`src/core/hard_caps.py`) that no configuration file can raise, such as 22 kV for the Spellman supplies and 95 A for the analyser magnet. A command past it is refused. | [Configuration files and the config editor](engineering/configuration.md#hard-caps) |
| hardware abort | The PLC's immediate shutdown, straight to **CESIUM COOLDOWN**, on `Src_Coolant_Flow_Low`, `Src_Body_Temp_High`, `Cesium_Oven_OverTemp`, `Filament_OverCurrent` or a sustained arc. | [The PLC program](how-it-works/plc.md#shutdown-sequences) |
| heartbeat | The regular message each service sends the service manager to show it is running. A service whose heartbeats stop for longer than its timeout is restarted. | [Services and messaging](how-it-works/services.md#heartbeats-and-restarts) |
| High Voltage Systems Control box | The box on the operator's desk, next to the HMI's screen, with the safety relay's key switch (**DISABLE**, **ENABLE**), its **START** button and an emergency switch-off. | [Safety rules](safety.md#the-high-voltage-systems-control-box) |
| HMI | The operator's control program (`python src/hmi.py`, or the launcher): it shows what the services report and sends your commands to them. It holds no machine state, so closing it doesn't stop anything. | [A tour of the HMI](getting-started/screen-tour.md) |
| interlock | A condition that must hold for something to be allowed, or that switches something off when it is lost. SINDIE has the safety relay's hardwired loop, and the PLC's permissives and latched faults. | [System overview](how-it-works/overview.md#three-layers-of-control) |
| ioniser | See **thermionic**. | [TESS Ion Source Control](panels/ion-source.md) |
| lane | One of up to four plots in the data viewer, stacked on one time axis (**Lane 1** to **Lane 4**). Each channel is drawn in the lane chosen for it. | [Using the data viewer](viewer/data-viewer.md#lanes-and-line-colours) |
| launcher | The program (`main_launcher.py`) that starts the HMI and the data viewer without PyCharm, starts and stops the services, and keeps them running from the Windows tray. | [Starting the software](getting-started/starting-the-software.md#the-launcher) |
| load guard | The source turbo pump's overload watch, in the turbo service: it warns when the pump's current stays above 1.2 A at full speed (the valve to its backing pump shut) or the pump runs too hot, and if its current doesn't fall back trips: the source is shut down and the caesium oven cooled. | [The source turbo's load guard](how-it-works/turbo-guard.md) |
| lease | See **control lease**. | [The Automation bar](panels/automation-bar.md#control-leases) |
| logger | The service (`service_logger`) that records the device services' telemetry 20 times a second, and writes it to a chunk every 15 minutes. | [Data logging and storage](how-it-works/data-logging.md#the-telemetry-logger) |
| machine state | The PLC's overall state, `ion_beam.system.machine_state`: 0 Init, 1 Safe/Not Ready, 2 Ready (relay permissive met), 3 Live/HV (relay closed), 99 Fault (a fault latched in word 0, 1, 2, 4 or 5). | [The PLC program](how-it-works/plc.md#machine-states) |
| marker | A note at a moment in time, with a colour, added with **ADD MARKER** in **Experiment Logging** or in the data viewer. It is saved as an event and drawn as a line in the data viewer. | [Experiment Logging](panels/experiment-logging.md) |
| mass calibration | The relation between the analyser magnet's current I and the ion mass: AMU = k (I + I_off)² / E, where E is the beam energy in eV. The **Mass Scan** panel fits k and I_off to known peaks, and **CALCULATE & SET AMPS** in **Beamline Optics** uses them. | [Mass Scan](panels/mass-scan.md#mass-calibration) |
| mass scan | Stepping the analyser magnet's current over a range while the SMU measures the beam current at each step, to find the masses in the beam. Started from the **Mass Scan** panel and run by the sequencer. | [Mass Scan](panels/mass-scan.md) |
| neutral trap | **Neutral Trap (Pos)** and **Neutral Trap (Neg)** in **Beamline Optics**: two Spellman supplies, not yet configured in `config/mpd_config.json`. | [Beamline Optics](panels/beamline-optics.md) |
| NPLC | Number of power-line cycles: how long the SMU integrates each reading, 20 ms per cycle; more cycles give quieter but slower readings. The HMI's readings use NPLC 1; a mass scan uses its own (10 unless changed) and puts the SMU back to 1 at the end. | [Faraday Cup & SMU](panels/faraday-cup-smu.md#the-current-measurement) |
| optimiser | A sweep of two chosen setpoints over a grid, measuring the Faraday cup current at each point, then putting both back or going to a point you pick on the heat map. Started from the **Optimiser** panel and run by the sequencer. | [Optimiser](panels/optimiser.md) |
| panel start button | The **START** button on the **High Voltage Systems Control** box, on the operator's desk: press it after **ENABLE SAFETY RELAY**, within 5 s, while the HMI's button counts down, for the safety relay to close. | [System Health](panels/system-health.md#switching-on-the-safety-relay) |
| permissive | A condition the PLC needs before it allows something, such as the relay permissive or the gate valve's open permissive. The PC sends the PLC one of them, the vacuum permissive (`ion_beam.source.chamber.stat_vac_ok_for_gv`). | [The PLC program](how-it-works/plc.md#the-enable-and-the-permissive) |
| permit | A service's statement, in its telemetry, of whether it would accept an operator command for a subsystem now (`<subsystem>.permit`), and why not (`permit_reason`). The HMI greys out the controls and shows the reason as `Disabled: <reason>`. | [Services and messaging](how-it-works/services.md#permits) |
| PLC | The Siemens S7-1200 programmable logic controller that runs the interlocks, latched faults and shutdown sequences, and drives the source supplies, the gate valve and the Faraday cup. The PC talks to it through DB10. | [The PLC program](how-it-works/plc.md) |
| readback | The value a device measures and reports, such as a supply's output voltage, as against the setpoint asked of it. Readback tags have the prefix `rb_`. | [System overview](how-it-works/overview.md#tag-names) |
| recipe | A list of steps that the sequencer runs: commands, waits, checks, routines and prompts. Recipes are made in the recipe builder, kept in `config/recipes/` and run from **Recipe Execution**. | [Recipe Execution and the recipe builder](panels/recipes.md) |
| relay permissive | The PLC's condition for passing the enable to the safety relay: compressed air, source coolant and magnet coolant OK, the gate valve open, no relay kill from a shutdown sequence, and no `Cesium_Oven_OverTemp`. | [The PLC program](how-it-works/plc.md#the-enable-and-the-permissive) |
| remote I/O | The PLC's input and output rack on the source deck, linked to the PLC by Profinet over fibre. Losing it, once the relay has been closed for 25 s, latches `Remote_IO_Lost` and starts shutdown scenario A. | [The PLC program](how-it-works/plc.md#the-remote-io-on-the-source-deck) |
| rollup | The minimum and maximum of every channel in 1-minute and 20-minute bins, written by the compactor next to each day file. The data viewer draws from them when it shows more than 24 hours. | [Data logging and storage](how-it-works/data-logging.md#how-the-data-viewer-reads-it) |
| routine | A built-in procedure a recipe step runs (a `ROUTINE_CALL` step): `psu_control` (**Switch on a supply and set it** in the recipe builder), `safety_relay_init` (**Bring the safety relay on**), `hv_conditioning` (**Ramp a supply up slowly**) or `mass_scan` (**Mass scan, then go to the peak**). | [Recipe Execution and the recipe builder](panels/recipes.md#procedures) |
| safety relay | The relay that has to be closed for high voltage: while it is open, the source deck and the Spellman supplies have no power and the PLC holds its supplies off. It closes only with the PLC's enable (**ENABLE SAFETY RELAY** in **System Health**), its hardwired loop made (doors shut, ESOs released, the key at **ENABLE**, the vacuum and the magnet coolant good) and **START** pressed on the **High Voltage Systems Control** box. | [Safety rules](safety.md#what-protects-you) |
| sequencer | The service (`service_sequencer`) that runs recipes, mass scans and the optimiser, outside the HMI, holding control leases on what it drives. | [Services and messaging](how-it-works/services.md) |
| service | One of the separate programs, started by the service manager, that each run a device or a job (for example `service_plc` or `service_alarms`), publish telemetry and take commands. | [Services and messaging](how-it-works/services.md#the-services) |
| service manager | The program (`python src/services/service_manager.py`) that starts every service, watches their heartbeats and restarts any that stops. The **Service Manager** panel shows what it reports. | [Service Manager](panels/service-manager.md) |
| setpoint | The value asked of a supply. A PLC supply has two: the requested setpoint the PC sends (`sp_requested_`) and the setpoint the PLC applies (`sp_actual_`), which can differ from it, for example during a shutdown sequence, an arc quench or auto-emission. | [The PLC program](how-it-works/plc.md#setpoint-arbitration-and-control-modes) |
| shelve | Stop an alarm being announced for 1 to 480 minutes, with a reason (**Shelve...** in the **Alarms** panel). It still follows its condition, and comes back when the time is up or it is unshelved. | [Alarms](panels/alarms.md#shelving) |
| shutdown sequence | The PLC's sequence that takes the source down: after the remote I/O is lost (scenario A), after the PC watchdog is lost or on a hardware abort (scenario B), or after **INITIATE SHUTDOWN** (scenario C). **SHUTDOWN SEQ** in **System Health** shows its step. | [The PLC program](how-it-works/plc.md#shutdown-sequences) |
| SINDIE | Surrey Implanter for Negative-ions, Defect Implantation, and Enrichment: the ion implanter this software runs. It used to be called IPIDS, a name still found in places in the code. | [SINDIE help](README.md) |
| slits | The object slits (before the analyser magnet) and the image slits (after it), each with a left and a right jaw, set by hand. The widths typed into **Beamline Optics** only go into the event log. | [Beamline Optics](panels/beamline-optics.md) |
| SMU | The Keysight B2900-series source-measure unit that biases the Faraday cup and measures the beam current, run by `service_smu`. The bias has a hard cap of ±200 V. | [Faraday Cup & SMU](panels/faraday-cup-smu.md) |
| source deck | The high-voltage part of the ion source, with the filament, thermionic and caesium oven supplies and the remote I/O. Both of its doors are interlocked: the gullwing door over the source body and the mesh door to the source trolley. | [Safety rules](safety.md) |
| Spellman supply | A Spellman MPD high-voltage supply, run by `service_spellman_mpd` over RS-485. Only the source einzel (unit 1) is configured; the beamline einzel and the neutral traps aren't yet. | [Services and messaging](how-it-works/services.md) |
| stale | Of a command: sent too long ago for the service to act on, so it is refused (`stale: sent <age> s ago (limit <limit> s)`); the sequencer sends its own again, and lease commands are never refused for it. Of telemetry: from a service that has gone quiet, so its values count as unknown and the HMI shows `---`; the **Vacuum System** panel instead keeps a gauge's or the turbo pump's last value, greyed, with a **STALE** chip saying how long ago it was read. | [Services and messaging](how-it-works/services.md#telemetry), [Vacuum System](panels/vacuum.md#readings-that-have-stopped) |
| steerer | The two beam steerers before the analyser magnet: the Y-steerer is electrostatic (set in V, through the PLC) and the X-steerer magnetic (set in A, through `service_steerer_magnet_psu`). **ENABLE STEERING** in **Beamline Optics** switches both. | [Beamline Optics](panels/beamline-optics.md) |
| stop conditions | What ends a recipe as failed as soon as it holds: a PLC fault latched, or a condition on a live reading. The sequencer checks those of the recipe and of every sub-recipe running; a reading they need without a live value for 3 s stops the recipe too. In the file: `fault_policy`. | [Recipe Execution and the recipe builder](panels/recipes.md#stop-conditions) |
| suppressed alarm | An alarm explained by another active alarm, for example each gauge's comms alarm while its controller's comms alarm is active. It is listed greyed and doesn't sound. | [Alarms](panels/alarms.md#suppressed-alarms) |
| tag | The name of one value in the system, such as `ion_beam.source.target.sp_requested_voltage`. Its prefix tells what it is: `rb_` readback, `sp_requested_` or `sp_actual_` setpoint, `stat_` status, `cmd_` command. | [System overview](how-it-works/overview.md#tag-names) |
| tag registry | The list of every tag with its source, unit, limits and labels, `config/system_tags.json`, built from the configuration files by `src/utils/build_registry.py`. The HMI's setpoint boxes take their ranges from it. | [Configuration files and the config editor](engineering/configuration.md#the-tag-registry) |
| target | The TESS's sputter target, also called the cathode, and the high-voltage supply that biases it (**Target** in **TESS Ion Source Control**, in kV and mA). Typically you aim for a target current of about 1 mA. | [TESS Ion Source Control](panels/ion-source.md) |
| telemetry | The values each service publishes every cycle. The HMI, the data viewer, the logger and other services listen to it; a service silent for 2 s or more (longer for one that publishes slowly) counts as stale. | [Services and messaging](how-it-works/services.md#telemetry) |
| TESS | Thermionic Emission Sputter Source: SINDIE's ion source, controlled from the **TESS Ion Source Control** panel. | [TESS Ion Source Control](panels/ion-source.md) |
| thermal model | The PLC's estimates of how hot the filament and the caesium oven are. While the oven's own reading isn't available (relay off or remote I/O lost), the panel shows the estimate, marked `~`. | [The PLC program](how-it-works/plc.md#thermal-models) |
| thermionic | The thermionic supply, also called the ioniser, set in mA in **TESS Ion Source Control**; typically 100 to 150 mA. Auto-emission can hold its current at the setpoint by adjusting the filament. | [TESS Ion Source Control](panels/ion-source.md) |
| tolerance | How far a readback may be from its setpoint, set per supply in `config/readback_tolerance.json`. A readback outside it for longer than its delay gets an amber outline and a TOLERANCE event: information, not a fault. | [How the controls behave](getting-started/controls-and-colours.md#readbacks-out-of-tolerance) |
| turbo pump | The source turbo pump, with a Leybold TURBOVAC frequency converter, run by `service_source_turbo` and shown in the **Vacuum System** panel. | [Vacuum System](panels/vacuum.md#source-turbo-pump) |
| VG1 to VG6 | The six vacuum gauges: **Source (VG1)**, **Beamline pre-magnet (VG2)**, **Beamline (VG3)**, **Endstation 1 (VG4)**, **Loadlock (VG5)** and **Endstation 2 (VG6)**. VG1 and VG2 decide whether the gate valve may open. | [Vacuum System](panels/vacuum.md#vacuum-gauges) |
| watchdog | The PLC service changes a value in the PLC every 0.5 s; if the PLC sees it unchanged for 3 s, it latches `PC_Watchdog_Timeout` and starts a shutdown sequence. The logger has a watchdog of its own: after 1 s of silence from a service it logs that service's values as unknown. | [The PLC program](how-it-works/plc.md#the-watchdog) |
| ZeroMQ | The messaging library the programs use to talk to each other on the lab PC: the services publish telemetry, and take and answer commands. | [Services and messaging](how-it-works/services.md) |
